# 💫 About Me:
### Data Scientist | AI Engineer | ML Engineer

I’m Marco Mo, a recent MSc graduate in Data Science and AI from TU Eindhoven.

I build machine learning and AI systems, with experience in NLP, energy disaggregation, and database optimization, as well as reinforcement learning. I’ve also worked on LLM-based applications such as a legal contract copilot.

I enjoy working across the full pipeline, from data and modeling to scalable solutions.
